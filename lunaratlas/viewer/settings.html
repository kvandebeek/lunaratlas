    Observing site (optional)

    It corrects the Moon's tilt for where you are on Earth (at most 1°). Leave it empty and the Earth's centre is used.

    Your location stays on this computer. It is stored in your LunarAtlas settings only: never sent anywhere, never written into exported images, their metadata or the info block. “Use my location” rounds it to 0.1° (about 10 km).
